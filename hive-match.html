Hive Match

How to play
  1. All the cards start face down. On your turn, flip two cards.
  2. If they are worth the same amount, you keep the pair and flip again. A pair is usually a number and a diagram, like 3/4 and a bar with 6 of 8 parts shaded.
  3. If they do not match, they flip back and the other player goes. Remember where things are.
  4. The player with the most pairs wins.
  5. Mild uses simple fractions. Medium adds improper fractions and mixed numbers. Spicy uses unsimplified fractions like 6/12, so 1/2 can match 6/12 or a diagram cut into 8 parts.
  6. Against the bot, the level also sets how good the bot's memory is.
